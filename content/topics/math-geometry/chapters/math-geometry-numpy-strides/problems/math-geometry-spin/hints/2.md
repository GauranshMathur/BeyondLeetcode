For an `n` by `n` matrix, ring `i` goes from 0 to `n / 2 - 1` (whole-number division), and `j` goes from `i` to `n - 2 - i`. The four items are `(i, j)`, `(j, n - 1 - i)`, `(n - 1 - i, n - 1 - j)` and `(n - 1 - j, i)`. Clockwise, `(i, j)` receives what is at `(n - 1 - j, i)`, which receives from `(n - 1 - i, n - 1 - j)`, which receives from `(j, n - 1 - i)`, which receives the old `(i, j)`. Keep the old `(i, j)` in a variable.
