The function makes four writes: `next`'s `prev` becomes the new node, the new node's `next` and `prev` become `next` and `prev`, and `prev`'s `next` becomes the new node. In an empty list `prev` and `next` are both the head, and the same four writes are correct. `show` starts at the head's `next` and follows `next` cells until it is back at the head.
