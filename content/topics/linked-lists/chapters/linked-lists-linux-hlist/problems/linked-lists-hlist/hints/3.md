`hadd N H`: `N.next` becomes the old first node; if there was one, its `pprev` becomes `N`; then the head cell holds `N` and `N.pprev` is `H`. `hadd_behind N X`: `N.next` is `X`'s next, `X`'s next becomes `N`, `N.pprev` is `X`, and if `N.next` is not 0 its `pprev` becomes `N`. Read what you need before you overwrite it.
